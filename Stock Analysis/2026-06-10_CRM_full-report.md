# Salesforce, Inc. (CRM) — Full Investment Report

**Data: yfinance + web, as of June 10, 2026 (intraday — today's volume may be partial)**

## Executive Summary

Salesforce is the dominant CRM/enterprise-workflow platform ($42.8B TTM revenue, 77.6% gross margins), and right now it's the cheapest it has ever traded: **11x forward earnings and a ~10% free-cash-flow yield** — multiples the market normally reserves for dying legacy software, attached to a company that just grew EPS 52%. The stock is **-32.4% YTD and -38% from its high**, trading $7 above its 52-week low, because the market is asking an existential question: *if AI agents replace human workers, who needs seat-based software licenses?* Q1 FY2027 (reported May 27) beat on earnings — GAAP EPS +52%, non-GAAP +50% — but the Q2 revenue guide missed, and that was enough to keep the stock pinned near its lows.

**Verdict: Watch — closer to a bottom than VST on valuation, but the bear thesis here is existential, not cyclical.** This has migrated from a growth stock to a value stock: ~9-10% organic revenue growth with spectacular margin expansion. The bull case is a multiple re-rating once Agentforce proves AI is accretive rather than cannibalistic; the bear case is a classic value trap where 11x is *fair* for a decelerating seat-count business. The chart just rejected a bounce at $181 and offers no entry signal yet.

## Fundamental Analysis

**The earnings machine is accelerating, not breaking.** Three-year trend (fiscal years ending January):

| | FY2023 | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | $31.4B | $34.9B | $37.9B | $41.5B |
| EBIT | $1.9B | $6.0B | $7.7B | **$8.9B** |
| Net Income | $0.2B | $4.1B | $6.2B | **$7.5B** |
| Free Cash Flow | $6.3B | $9.5B | $12.4B | **$14.4B** |

That's the activist-driven margin story playing out: GAAP operating margin went from ~6% to 21.8% in three years while FCF more than doubled. Latest quarter EPS +52% YoY. Capex is trivial ($594M — capital-light), so nearly all operating cash converts to FCF.

**The top line is the problem.** Annual revenue growth has settled at ~9.5%. The latest quarter's +13.3% YoY is flattered by the ~$8B Informatica acquisition (which also explains debt jumping to $17.2B against $7.3B cash — Salesforce was historically net-cash). Organic growth near single digits is what's feeding the "ex-growth" narrative, and the Q2 revenue guidance miss reinforced it. Note: the current ratio of 0.79 looks alarming but isn't — SaaS companies carry huge deferred-revenue balances in current liabilities; it's prepaid subscription cash, not a bill coming due.

**Valuation context.** CRM's forward P/E has historically ranged 25–60x; the S&P trades ~20x. At 11x forward / 4.1x EV/revenue for 77% gross-margin recurring revenue, the market is pricing in growth decay toward zero. Mean analyst target $255 (+49%), though the $160 low target sits right at the current price — someone on the street already thinks this is fair value.

## Bull vs Bear

**Bull:** (1) Historic valuation floor — 11x forward earnings, 10% FCF yield, PEG 0.87; a mere re-rating to 15x on FY2028 estimates is a $230+ stock; (2) EPS and FCF compounding 30-50% while you wait, with buyback capacity of $14B/yr at depressed prices; (3) the AI-disruption fear may invert — agents need clean customer data and workflow context, which is exactly what Salesforce + Data Cloud + Informatica own; Agentforce is consumption-priced, hedging the seat model; (4) 53 analysts at mean $255.

**Bear:** (1) The seat-based-pricing question is legitimate and unfalsifiable for now — if organic growth decays to low single digits, 11x isn't cheap, it's *correct* (see IBM 2013-2020); (2) growth is already decelerating and management's own Q2 guide missed; (3) Informatica re-opens the big-M&A capital-allocation worry and erased the net-cash balance sheet; (4) the whole legacy-SaaS complex is de-rating together — no idiosyncratic catalyst until Agentforce revenue shows up big in the numbers; (5) chart broken, every rally sold.

## Recommendation

**Rating: Hold / Watchlist. Conviction: Medium. 12–24 month fair value: $200–255.**

Against a growth-oriented framework this is a near-miss: valuation and EPS momentum clear the bar easily (19.8x trailing; 52% EPS growth), but **~9-10% organic revenue growth makes this a value/re-rating play, not a growth compounder** — the 30% CAGR outcome requires the multiple to recover, which requires the AI-disruption narrative to break. That's a sentiment catalyst you can't schedule. Per "if there's any doubt, there's no doubt" — there's doubt about the *story*, not the numbers, and stories are what move multiples.

Practical plan:

1. **Trigger A (trend repair):** daily close above **$182** (clears the MA20/50 lid where the last bounce died) — enter with stop below $170; first target $211, then $230.
2. **Trigger B (capitulation):** a high-volume flush into **$160–164** that reverses (close back above $164) — tighter risk, stop below $157.
3. **No-man's land ($165–181):** do nothing. Mid-range entries in downtrends are how you donate to the market.
4. **Catalyst calendar:** Q2 FY2027 earnings ~late August, Dreamforce (Sept/Oct) for Agentforce proof points. If Agentforce/Data Cloud numbers inflect, the re-rate can be violent — that's the moment to size up, even 15% off the lows.

**vs. VST (analyzed same day):** both are -30%+ broken charts with cheap forward multiples, but they're different bets — VST is a *cyclical/thematic* de-rating with accelerating fundamentals (43% revenue growth); CRM is a *secular-fear* de-rating with decelerating top line but a much fatter cash-flow cushion. VST recovers if the AI-power theme re-heats; CRM needs to actually disprove a disruption thesis. For a single knife-catch on the watchlist, VST has the cleaner fundamental momentum; CRM has the cheaper valuation floor.
